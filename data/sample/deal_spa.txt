ILLUSTRATIVE SYNTHETIC CORPUS - Deal Advisory & Corporate Finance (fictional contract; for demonstration only)

Share Purchase Agreement - Project Meridian (Illustrative)
This Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) is entered into between Alpha Holdings Pvt Ltd (the Seller) and Zenith Industries Ltd (the Purchaser) for the acquisition of 100% of the equity share capital of Meridian Components Pvt Ltd (the Target) for an enterprise value of INR 850 crore.

Clause 9 - Indemnities (Illustrative)
Under Clause 9.1 the Seller shall indemnify the Purchaser against Losses arising from breach of Warranties. Clause 9.2 provides that the Seller's aggregate liability is capped at 25% of the Purchase Price, except for Fundamental Warranties and Tax Indemnity under Clause 9.4, which are capped at 100% of the Purchase Price. A basket (deductible) of INR 2 crore applies to each claim under Clause 9.1. Indemnity claims must be notified within 24 months of Closing, and within 6 years for the Tax Indemnity.

Clause 11 - Material Adverse Change (Illustrative)
Clause 11.1 defines a Material Adverse Change (MAC) as any event that reduces the consolidated EBITDA of the Target by more than 15% or results in loss of the Target's key customer Orion Motors, which contributes 40% of revenue. If a MAC occurs between signing and Closing, the Purchaser may terminate the SPA under Clause 11.3 without liability. The Seller disclosed a pending Income Tax reassessment of INR 40 crore related to Section 148, which is excluded from the MAC definition but covered by the Tax Indemnity in Clause 9.4.

Regulatory conditions (Illustrative)
Because Zenith Industries Ltd is a non-resident-controlled entity, the acquisition is subject to FEMA pricing guidelines: the share price must not be lower than the fair value determined by a registered valuer under the Companies Act 2013 valuation standards. Clause 5.2 makes receipt of the Competition Commission of India approval and filing of Form FC-TRS with the Reserve Bank of India conditions precedent to Closing. Any foreign contribution received by the Target is subject to FCRA compliance, which the Seller warrants in Clause 8.7.
